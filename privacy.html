    Privacy Policy

    StegZ privacy policy.

    StegZ runs on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry,

    and it does not transmit your files, passwords, or filenames anywhere.

    **The only network activity** is an update check: on start-up and about every

    20 minutes while the app is open, StegZ downloads two small public files

    (`latest.json` and `announcement.json`) from the project's GitHub-hosted

    website, and, if a newer version exists, downloads the installer from the

    project's GitHub Releases page. These are ordinary downloads: nothing about

    you or your files is sent, although GitHub, like any web host, may see your IP

    address in its normal server logs. You can turn off automatic update downloads

    in Settings.

    • All hiding/extraction happens locally, in memory and on your local disk.
    • Your "recent files" list is stored only in a local file on your own machine

    and is never uploaded.

    • Passwords you set are never stored by StegZ in any form - if you forget a

    password, your protected content cannot be recovered by StegZ or anyone else.

    • Uninstalling StegZ removes its settings; it does not remove files you

    created with it.
