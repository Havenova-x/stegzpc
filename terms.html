Hiding files in pictures

This feature relies on programs and operating systems ignoring data placed after a picture's end marker. Many websites, messaging apps and social platforms re-compress pictures on upload, which destroys the hidden data. StegZ is not a transmission service and can't guarantee that hidden data survives being uploaded to third-party services.

Passwords

Password protection uses standard AES-256 encryption. If you forget your password, your data can't be recovered. There is no backdoor.

Self-destruct

Self-destruct permanently and irreversibly destroys an archive's contents once its condition is met. This is intentional. It can't be undone, and support can't restore it. It only ever affects the exact file you created.
