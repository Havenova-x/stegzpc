    • StegZ is a general-purpose file-archiving and privacy utility, similar in

    • You are solely responsible for the legality of the files you store,

    hide, or share using StegZ. Do not use StegZ to store, hide, or

    distribute illegal content.

    • StegZ's "hidden in image" feature relies on viewers/OSes ignoring data

    appended after an image's end marker. Many websites, messaging apps, and

    social platforms re-compress images on upload, which destroys hidden data.

    StegZ is not a transmission tool and does not guarantee hidden data

    survives upload to third-party services.
