Questions

Is StegZ really free?

Yes. StegZ is free to download and use. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, and Android phones and tablets. Files you make on one open on the other.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your files are processed on your device. The app only goes online to check for updates and news.

What happens if I forget my password?

The contents can't be recovered. There is no backdoor, by design, so keep your password somewhere safe.

Will hidden files survive social media?

Usually not. Many apps and sites re-compress pictures when you upload them, which removes hidden data. Send the picture as a file or email attachment instead.

Is the download safe?

Installers come straight from the official StegZ releases on GitHub. The download page shows the file's SHA-256 checksum so you can verify it.

What is a .stegz file?

StegZ's own compact archive. It carries a checksum, an optional password and optional self-destruct rules.
