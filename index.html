    Questions

    Is StegZ free?

    Yes. No ads, accounts or tracking.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. Only update checks use the internet.

    What is a .stegz file?

    StegZ's own compact archive with a checksum, optional password and optional self-destruct.

    Why do I also see 'steg', 'stegs', 'stegsz'?

    They are common spellings people search for - they all lead to StegZ.
